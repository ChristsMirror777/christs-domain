#### Gain Wisdom Draft 1

Everyone feels like they are being dragged around by life at times. Most people have many authority figures in their lives that tell them what to do and how to live. We must submit to these figures because God has anointed them to be in that position over us, and as we obey them we obey Christ - Ephesians 6:5 NIV, Romans 13:1 NIV

Though it is good to humble ourselves before authorities. Sometimes we don't like the way things are being done. This server focuses a lot on sovereignty, because we are all kings and queens in Christ. A royal priesthood - Peter 2:9

Sometimes we know exactly what we want and it doesn't match up with what authorities push on us. You're allowed to have ambition, and to want freedom from these systems. So long as it isn't selfish Philippians 2:3-4

If you feel your and others needs are not being met, I encourage you to do something about it. But understand that following that ambition will yield nothing if God sees you are doing it from a wrong heart. Submit to authorities, build up The Kingdom, and become sovereign in Christ. Fulfilling all duties to Christ, authorities, and family. It's slow, it can be painfully slow. But through faith it will be done.
